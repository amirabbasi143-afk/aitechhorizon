I Tested AI Resume Builders: Which One Actually Got Me Interviews?

Published on September 2, 2026 | Category: AI Tools | By: AI Tech Horizon Team

Getting your resume past Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) is a nightmare. Recently, a wave of AI resume builders claims to optimize your CV to beat these robots. But do they work in the real world?

Our team took a mediocre resume of a junior software developer and used three different AI tools to upgrade it. We then applied to 50 jobs using each version (150 applications total) to see which one resulted in actual callbacks. Here is what we found.

1. Rezi.ai (The Winner)

Our Experience: Rezi is built specifically to beat ATS systems. It analyzed our target job description and told us exactly which keywords we were missing. It also used AI to rewrite boring bullet points into "Action + Result" formats.

The Results: Out of 50 applications, the Rezi resume got 6 interview requests (12% success rate). The formatting was clean, machine-readable, and highly effective.

2. Kickresume AI

Our Experience: Kickresume has beautiful, highly graphic templates and uses OpenAI to write your summary. However, ATS systems hate complex graphics and columns.

The Results: Out of 50 applications, this resume got only 2 interview requests (4% success rate). While it looked visually stunning to a human, we suspect the ATS bots couldn't parse the fancy layout properly.

3. The Manual ChatGPT Method

Our Experience: We pasted the resume and job description into ChatGPT with the prompt: "Rewrite this resume to match the keywords in this job description." We then pasted the text into a plain Word document.

The Results: Out of 50 applications, this plain-text resume got 4 interview requests (8% success rate). It proved that simple formatting combined with targeted keywords is highly effective, even without fancy software.

Our Conclusion

If you want a dedicated platform, Rezi.ai is worth the money. However, if you are on a budget, pasting your resume into a plain Word document and using ChatGPT to inject job-specific keywords is a highly effective, free alternative.
