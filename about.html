About AI Tech Horizon

Demystifying Artificial Intelligence through hands-on testing, objective comparisons, and practical real-world workflows.

Our Mission

AI Tech Horizon was founded to provide clear, actionable, and hype-free guidance in an era of rapid technological disruption. Rather than merely reporting on sensationalized AI announcements, we test generative tools, automate daily workflows, and publish verifiable benchmarks so that professionals, students, and businesses can understand what genuinely works.

Editorial Principles

We adhere to strict editorial standards designed to protect reader trust and maintain the highest level of factual integrity:

  • Hands-On Verification: We do not publish reviews based on marketing brochures. Every software evaluation is grounded in actual operational testing.
  • Ethical Technology Use: We strictly oppose deceptive AI use cases, such as plagiarism evasion tools, unverified financial prediction bots, or misleading automated content schemes.
  • Transparency: When an AI model demonstrates limitations, latency issues, or hallucinations, we document them prominently.
  • Editorial Independence: Our reviews and tutorials are not dictated by software vendor sponsorship.

How We Test AI Tools

Our benchmark framework evaluates AI applications across four core dimensions:

  1. Accuracy & Reasoning: Evaluating the model's factual consistency and logical coherence on complex instructions.
  2. Speed & Latency: Measuring time-to-first-token and end-to-end task completion times.
  3. Ergonomics & Integration: Assessing API documentation, UI responsiveness, and compatibility with everyday workplace software.
  4. Cost & Value: Analyzing whether subscription tiers justify the return on productivity investment.
