How to Make Money Testing AI Websites and Apps

Published on August 29, 2026 | Category: Make Money

Did you know you can get paid to try out new AI tools? With thousands of AI startups launching every month, developers desperately need real human feedback to improve their platforms. This is where AI Website Testing comes in.

What is AI Website Testing?

Companies will pay you to record your screen and voice while you navigate their new AI app. They want to know: Is the UI confusing? Does the AI generate accurate results? Were you able to find the pricing page easily?

Top Platforms to Find Testing Jobs

1. UserTesting: The biggest platform in the world. You can earn $10 for every 20-minute test. They have a dedicated section for tech and AI startups.

2. Remotasks / Outlier: These platforms pay you specifically to train AI models. You might be asked to evaluate two different responses from an AI chatbot and rate which one is better.

3. DataAnnotation Tech: Highly lucrative platform for coders and writers to test AI models. You can earn between $20 to $40 an hour by writing prompts and rating the AI's response.

Conclusion

Testing AI apps is one of the easiest side hustles to start. You don't need any special skills—just a laptop, a microphone, and the ability to speak your thoughts out loud. Sign up for multiple platforms today to maximize your earning potential.
