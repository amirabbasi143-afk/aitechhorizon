How to Test Websites and Apps With AI: A Beginner's Guide

Updated on September 4, 2026 | Category: Make Money | By: AI Tech Horizon Editorial Team

Website and app testing (Quality Assurance or QA) is a legitimate freelance career. Companies pay real people to navigate their software to find bugs before the public does. While AI cannot do this job for you, it can be an excellent assistant for writing test cases and documenting bugs.

Here is a realistic guide on how this process actually works and what skills you need to get started.

What is Website/App Testing?

As a tester, you are not writing code. You are given a set of instructions (a test case), such as: "Try to add an item to the cart, apply a discount code, and proceed to checkout." Your job is to follow those steps exactly and document any errors, broken links, or design flaws you encounter.

How AI Helps in the Testing Process

  • Writing Test Cases: If a client asks you to test a login page, you can ask ChatGPT: "Generate a list of 10 edge-case test scenarios for a standard login page." It will suggest things you might forget, like testing SQL injection characters or extremely long passwords.
  • Documenting Bugs: Writing clear bug reports is crucial. You can describe the issue roughly to AI, and ask it to format it professionally into "Steps to Reproduce," "Expected Behavior," and "Actual Behavior."

Required Skills and Tools

You do not need a computer science degree, but you do need:

  • Extreme attention to detail.
  • Basic knowledge of taking screenshots and screen recordings (using tools like Loom or Snagit).
  • Familiarity with bug tracking software (like Jira or Trello).
  • A reliable internet connection and ideally both a smartphone and a laptop.

Freelancing Platforms and Realistic Earnings

You can find testing gigs on platforms like UTest, TesterWork, Upwork, and Fiverr. However, managing expectations is important. Testing is rarely a "get rich quick" scheme. Beginners might make anywhere from $5 to $20 per test cycle, depending on the platform and complexity. It is an excellent side hustle, but replacing a full-time income requires building a long-term reputation and moving into automated testing (using tools like Selenium).

Conclusion

Website testing is a detail-oriented job that requires patience. By using AI to help brainstorm edge cases and write professional bug reports, you can deliver higher quality work to your clients and slowly increase your hourly rate over time.
