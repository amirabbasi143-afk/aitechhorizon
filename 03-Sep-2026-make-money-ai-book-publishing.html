How We Published an AI-Written Book on Amazon KDP in 48 Hours

Published on September 3, 2026 | Category: Make Money | By: AI Tech Horizon Team

Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) has been flooded with AI-generated books. Many gurus claim it's an easy way to make passive income, while critics say Amazon will ban your account. To find the truth, our team attempted to write, illustrate, and publish a children's book in exactly 48 hours.

Here is our exact workflow, our challenges, and our real-world results.

Day 1: The Writing and Editing Process

We decided on a children's book about a little robot learning about emotions. We used Claude 3.5 for the writing, as it tends to be more creative and less robotic than ChatGPT.

Our Experience: The first draft was terrible. It lacked pacing. We had to spend 3 hours acting as a "Director," telling the AI to rewrite specific pages, adjust the rhyme scheme, and simplify the vocabulary. Lesson learned: AI cannot write a good book in one click. You must co-write it.

Day 1 (Night): AI Illustrations

We used Midjourney v6 to generate the illustrations. The hardest part of AI book generation is keeping the character looking the same on every page.

Our Experience: We used Midjourney's Character Reference (--cref) feature, which was a lifesaver. We uploaded a base image of our robot and applied it to every prompt. It took about 4 hours to generate 15 consistent, high-quality images.

Day 2: Formatting and Publishing

We used Canva to drag and drop our Midjourney images and paste our Claude text. Canva exported the perfect PDF format required for Amazon KDP.

When uploading to Amazon, we faced the new "AI Declaration" checkbox. We strictly followed the rules and checked "Yes, this contains AI-generated text and images." Lying here is what gets accounts banned.

The Final Result

Exactly 42 hours after we started, we hit "Publish." 12 hours later, Amazon approved the book, and it was live on the Kindle store.

Did we make money? In the first week, we sold exactly 3 copies (mostly to friends testing the link). The truth is, publishing an AI book is easy. Marketing an AI book is the actual hard part. Unless you run TikTok ads or Amazon PPC, your AI book will sit unseen among millions of others.
