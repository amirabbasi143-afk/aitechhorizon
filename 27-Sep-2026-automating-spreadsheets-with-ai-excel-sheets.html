Automating Spreadsheets with AI: Practical Excel and Google Sheets Guide

By AI Tech Horizon Editorial Team • Updated 27 Sep 2026 • 6 min read
Quick Summary & Takeaway:

Learn how to use AI to generate complex nested formulas (XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH), clean dirty unstructured text, and write custom Google Apps Script macros without prior programming experience.

Beyond Manual Cell Entry

Spreadsheet software remains the backbone of modern business operations. Yet millions of professionals waste countless hours on tedious data cleanup: splitting names, extracting dates from messy invoices, and debugging broken nested formulas. AI transforms spreadsheets from static grids into dynamic automated engines.

1. Generating Complex Nested Formulas

Instead of struggling through legacy syntax for multi-condition lookups, provide the AI with your exact column names and describe your objective in plain English.

2. Regex Pattern Matching and Data Extraction

Extracting phone numbers, transaction IDs, or postal codes from messy imported customer notes used to require specialized regular expression skills. Modern AI models can write perfect REGEXEXTRACT formulas in seconds, ensuring clean tabular columns for your business intelligence reports.

3. Automated Google Apps Script Macros

Need your sheet to automatically email weekly PDF summaries to department heads? Generative AI can generate complete Google Apps Script or Excel VBA code that connects your spreadsheets directly to your email client and external APIs.
